  • The Problem

    Everyone deserves a voice that is their own

    For people who do not have the ability to speak, like the late Stephen Hawking, Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) technology is a lifeline to the world.

    The problem: English and other major languages have plenty of Text-to-Speech (TTS) models. Under-resourced languages often have few, or none. Our project helps change that.

  • Our sponsor

    Dr. Benjamin Tucker

    Professor at Northern Arizona University, specializing in Natural Language Processing (NLP) and speech synthesis.

    Research focus: low-resource languages

    • South African English (SAE)
    • Afrikaans
    • isiXhosa

  • The challenge

    A research tool that stopped working

    Where it stood

    Dr. Tucker's previous text to speech system relied on NVIDIA's Tacotron2 implementation, which has been unmaintained since 2020 and no longer functions.

    What we're building

    A modern, maintainable replacement that keeps his research moving forward by allowing text to speech model creation and use.

  • Our solution

    Two components

    01

    Training pipeline

    A streamlined, modular framework for training TTS models without having to manage the system's underlying complexity.

    02

    Web application

    Hosts the trained models. Allows for speech generation without requiring any code or command-line interface.
