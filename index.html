Background

Speech is an important facet of many peoples lives both as a means for communcication and for engaging in the cuture(s) that they are a part of. For those with disabilities that affect their speech it can be difficult to integrate into their communities. To accomodate these disabilities, many choose to employ some form of speech synthesis. For under-resourced languages, however, there might not be a model capable of producing speech in their own language.
